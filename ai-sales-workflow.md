# AI Sales Workflow

## Overview

This project demonstrates how I use AI to support sales development tasks and improve productivity.

## AI-Assisted Workflow

### 1. Lead Research

Use AI to summarize company information and identify potential customer pain points before outreach.

### 2. Email Drafting

Use AI to generate personalized outreach emails that can be reviewed and edited before sending.

### 3. Call Preparation

Use AI to create discovery questions tailored to a prospect's industry and business needs.

### 4. Follow-Up Messages

Use AI to draft professional follow-up emails after sales calls while maintaining a personalized tone.

### 5. Meeting Summaries

Use AI to summarize meeting notes and identify action items for future follow-up.

## Tools

- ChatGPT
- Google Workspace
- HubSpot
- Salesforce

## What I Learned

Building this workflow helped me understand how AI can improve efficiency, save time on repetitive tasks, and support more personalized customer engagement while keeping human judgment at the center of the sales process.
