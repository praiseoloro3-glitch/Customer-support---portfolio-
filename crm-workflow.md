# CRM Workflow

## Lead Management Process

### 1. Lead Capture

- Record new leads from phone calls, referrals, customer inquiries, and outreach activities.
- Ensure customer information is accurate and complete.

### 2. Lead Qualification

- Identify customer needs.
- Assess interest and buying potential.
- Prioritize qualified leads for follow-up.

### 3. Follow-Up

- Schedule follow-up calls and meetings.
- Send reminders and maintain consistent communication.
- Update customer interactions after every conversation.

### 4. Pipeline Management

- Track every lead through each stage of the sales process.
- Monitor progress and identify opportunities requiring additional engagement.
- Keep records updated to ensure accurate reporting.

### 5. Relationship Building

- Maintain regular contact with customers.
- Provide timely responses to questions.
- Build trust through professional communication and consistent support.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep customer records accurate.
- Follow up consistently.
- Listen actively to customer needs.
- Communicate professionally.
- Focus on building long-term relationships.
